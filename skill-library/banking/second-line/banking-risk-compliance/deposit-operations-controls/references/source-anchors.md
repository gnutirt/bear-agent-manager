## Funds availability

- **Regulation CC — Availability of Funds and Collection of Checks, 12 CFR Part 229**. Federal Reserve Board. Subpart B (funds availability) — §229.10 next-day availability for specified deposits; §229.12 schedule for other deposits; §229.13 exceptions including new-account, large-deposit, redeposited-check, repeated-overdraft, reasonable-cause-to-doubt-collectibility, and emergency-conditions exceptions; §229.13(g) exception-hold notice content and timing. Subpart C (collection of checks) — interbank check-collection rules. Subpart D (substitute checks under Check 21) — §229.51 substitute-check legal equivalence; §229.54 expedited recredit for consumers. §229.21 civil liability and recordkeeping requirements. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-II/subchapter-A/part-229 .

## Truth in savings (deposit account disclosures)

- **Regulation DD — Truth in Savings, 12 CFR Part 1030**. CFPB (transferred from FRB Reg DD effective 2011). §1030.3 general disclosure requirements; §1030.4 account disclosures (content and timing — before account opening); §1030.5 subsequent disclosures (change in terms, fee-change notice timing); §1030.6 periodic statement disclosures including aggregate fees for overdraft and returned items; §1030.7 payment of interest; §1030.8 advertising including APY accuracy and triggering-term rules; §1030.9 enforcement and recordkeeping (24-month retention); §1030.11 additional disclosure requirements for institutions advertising the payment of overdrafts. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1030 .

- **CFPB Compliance Aid (small-entity compliance guide) on Reg DD** [verify current edition]. Plain-language interpretive aid. Cite the underlying §1030 section in the obligation-source column, not the guide. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/deposit-accounts-resources/truth-savings-act/ .

## Consumer EFT and prepaid

- **Regulation E — Electronic Fund Transfers, 12 CFR Part 1005**. CFPB. §1005.2 definitions including "consumer", "account", "electronic fund transfer", "unauthorised electronic fund transfer"; §1005.6 consumer liability for unauthorised EFTs (the $50 / $500 / unlimited tiers based on timeliness of consumer notice — within two business days, within 60 days, beyond 60 days); §1005.7 initial disclosures content and timing; §1005.8(a) change-in-terms notice [verify exact paragraph]; §1005.9 receipts at electronic terminals and periodic statements; §1005.10 preauthorised transfers including the right to stop payment; §1005.11 procedures for resolving errors — error-notice intake, the 10-business-day investigation window, the provisional-credit duty if investigation is not concluded within that window (extended to 20 business days for new accounts in certain conditions), the 45-day full-investigation window (extended to 90 days for POS and foreign-initiated transactions), and the notification-of-results duty; §1005.13 administrative enforcement and recordkeeping (24-month retention); §1005.18 prepaid accounts including pre-acquisition disclosure (short form and long form), error-resolution rules adapted for prepaid, and the credit-feature rules under §1005.18(g) — the prepaid sub-regime is discrete and must not be folded into general consumer-EFT controls. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1005 .

## CIP and beneficial ownership

- **Customer Identification Program rule for banks, 31 CFR §1020.220**. FinCEN. §1020.220(a)(2) identity-verification procedures (documentary and non-documentary); §1020.220(a)(3) recordkeeping (5-year retention from account closure); §1020.220(a)(4) comparison with government lists of known or suspected terrorists; §1020.220(a)(5) customer notice. The matrix cites this regulation as the obligation source; the FFIEC BSA / AML Examination Manual CIP section is interpretive guidance and goes in the secondary anchor row. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1020/subpart-B/section-1020.220 .

- **Beneficial-ownership rule for legal-entity customers, 31 CFR §1010.230**. FinCEN. Collection of beneficial-ownership information (each beneficial owner with 25% or more equity, plus one control person) at account opening for legal-entity customers; certification by the legal entity; recordkeeping. The bank rule under §1010.230 is distinct from the FinCEN beneficial-ownership reporting rule under the Corporate Transparency Act (which is reporting-company-facing, not bank-facing). The CTA reporting-rule docket has been active and litigation has affected the operative state; verify current state on the analysis date. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-D/section-1010.230 .

- **FFIEC BSA / AML Examination Manual — CIP section** [verify current edition and section]. Interagency guidance; cite the underlying 31 CFR rule in the obligation-source column. https://bsaaml.ffiec.gov/manual .

- **FFIEC BSA / AML Examination Manual — Customer Due Diligence and Beneficial Ownership Requirements for Legal Entity Customers section** [verify current edition and section]. Interagency guidance on the §1010.230 rule. https://bsaaml.ffiec.gov/manual .

## Deposit insurance and FBO accounts

- **Federal Deposit Insurance Act and FDIC regulations on insurance of accounts, 12 CFR Part 330**. FDIC. §330.5 recordkeeping requirements for deposits maintained in fiduciary capacities (the recordkeeping conditions for pass-through coverage on fiduciary and custodial accounts) [verify section labels]; §330.7 accounts held by an agent, nominee, guardian, custodian, or conservator; §330.10 revocable trust accounts. For sponsor-bank fintech-program FBO accounts, the bank's pass-through deposit-insurance posture turns on whether the recordkeeping conditions are satisfied — without compliant recordkeeping, the FBO depositors lose pass-through coverage. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-330 .

- **FDIC Part 328 final rule (December 2023) with effective-date extensions through 2024-2025** — verify current effective date for the engagement. **12 CFR Part 328 Subpart B** prohibits misrepresenting deposit-insurance coverage and provides standards for non-bank entities (including fintech program operators) holding out deposit insurance. The bank's review obligation extends to customer-facing surfaces of any sponsor-bank fintech program where the bank is named as the insured depository institution. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-328/subpart-B .

- **Joint Statement on Banks' Arrangements with Third Parties to Deliver Bank Deposit Products and Services** (FDIC / OCC / FRB, July 25, 2024) [verify exact title and date — this is the operative statement, not the 2023 Request for Information]. Supervisory guidance on FBO subledger reconciliation, end-customer reconciliation, deposit-insurance representation review, customer-facing FDIC framing, and operational-resilience expectations on the program operator. Supervisory guidance, not regulation; phrase obligation-source as "supervisory guidance" and cite the underlying regulation (consumer-EFT, truth-in-savings, 12 CFR Part 330, Part 328 Subpart B) for the binding text. https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2024/ [verify exact FIL number].

## Garnishments, levies, and federal-benefit-payment protection

- **Garnishment of accounts containing federal benefit payments, 31 CFR Part 212**. Treasury. Lookback procedure (two-month lookback from receipt of garnishment order); protected-amount calculation; notice-to-account-holder content and timing. Applies across charter types where the account holds federal-benefit direct deposits (Social Security, SSI, Veterans Affairs, Federal Railroad Retirement, Federal Civil Service Retirement, Federal employee compensation). https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-II/part-212 .

- **State garnishment, IRS levy (26 USC §6332), and state-tax-levy rules** — state-by-state for state garnishment and state tax levies; verify the bank's state footprint. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6332 (IRS levy).

## Escheatment and dormant accounts

- **State unclaimed-property statutes** — state-by-state. Most states adopt some version of the Revised Uniform Unclaimed Property Act (RUUPA, 2016) or earlier Uniform Unclaimed Property Act (1995) [verify state-by-state adoption]. Dormancy classification triggers (typically 3 to 5 years of customer inactivity), customer-contact attempt cadence, and state escheatment-filing schedules vary by state. https://www.uniformlaws.org/committees/community-home?CommunityKey=4b7c796a-f158-47bc-b5b1-f3f9a6e404fa .

## Privacy and information security (cross-cutting; carry the underlying regulation here)

- **Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act Privacy Rule / Reg P, 12 CFR Part 1016**. CFPB. Privacy notice content and delivery; opt-out mechanics on information sharing with non-affiliated third parties. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1016 .

- **GLBA Safeguards Rule, 16 CFR Part 314** as amended (FTC, December 2021 amendments effective 2023). §314.4(f) service-provider obligations; §314.4(h) incident-response. Applies to non-bank financial institutions; bank-affiliate fintechs operating as financial institutions in their own right may be subject; verify per entity. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-314 .

- **Interagency Guidelines Establishing Information Security Standards** (12 CFR Part 30 Appendix B for OCC, 12 CFR Part 208 Appendix D-2 for FRB state-member banks, 12 CFR Part 364 Appendix B for FDIC) [verify part labels by charter]. Information-security program expectations applicable to banks. https://www.fdic.gov/regulations/safety/manual/ .

## OCC Heightened Standards (covered banks only)

- **12 CFR Part 30 Appendix D — OCC Guidelines Establishing Heightened Standards for Certain Large Insured National Banks, Insured Federal Savings Associations, and Insured Federal Branches** [verify current section labels]. Applies to covered banks (generally $50B+ avg total consolidated assets). The deposit-operations control matrix ties into the operational-risk and risk-governance framework where the bank is covered. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-30/appendix-Appendix%20D%20to%20Part%2030 .
