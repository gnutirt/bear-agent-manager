# Source anchors — credit-risk-governance

The named, dated public regulatory sources this skill anchors to for second-line credit risk governance review at US banks. SKILL.md cites this file by path and does not restate excerpts. Mark `[verify]` where the published source uses a section label or edition date the skill cannot confirm at write time.

## Credit administration — FRB and interagency

- **Interagency Guidance on Credit Risk Review Systems** (May 8, 2020). Joint FRB, FDIC, OCC, NCUA, and CFPB guidance, published by each agency under its own number: FRB SR 20-13 (May 8, 2020); OCC Bulletin 2020-44; FDIC FIL-50-2020. Replaces the prior 2006 Interagency Policy Statement on the Allowance for Loan and Lease Losses sections on the credit-risk review system. Sets independence, scope, frequency, and second-line challenge expectations on first-line risk-rating decisions. Anchors section 7 (second-line challenge of first-line lending decisions). https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2013.htm — FRB SR 20-13; https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2020/bulletin-2020-44.html — OCC Bulletin 2020-44; https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2020/fil20050.html — FDIC FIL-50-2020.
- **FRB Bank Holding Company Supervision Manual** (current edition) [verify current edition and section]. FRB's consolidated supervision framing for credit risk at the BHC level. https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/supervision_manual.htm.
- **FRB Commercial Bank Examination Manual** (current edition) [verify current edition and section]. FRB's examination process for state-member banks, including credit-administration sections. https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/files/cbem.pdf [verify URL].
- **FRB SR 06-17 — Interagency Policy Statement on the Allowance for Loan and Lease Losses** (December 13, 2006) [verify continued effectiveness post-CECL, the underlying policy statement was largely superseded by ACL guidance]. https://www.federalreserve.gov/boarddocs/srletters/2006/SR0617.htm.

## Concentration governance — Commercial Real Estate

- **Interagency Guidance on Concentrations in Commercial Real Estate Lending** (December 12, 2006), 71 FR 74580. Establishes the 100% of total capital construction-and-development screen and the 300% of total capital total-CRE screen as monitoring triggers for enhanced risk-management expectations (not regulatory caps). Anchors section 4 (concentration governance) for any bank with material CRE exposure. https://www.federalreserve.gov/boarddocs/srletters/2007/SR0701.htm [verify URL — also OCC OCC-2006-46 / FDIC FIL-104-2006].
- **Interagency Statement on Prudent Risk Management for Commercial Real Estate Lending** (December 18, 2015), FDIC FIL-62-2015. Joint statement reaffirming the 2006 framework, emphasising heightened expectations for banks above the 300% screen. https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2015/fil15062.html.

## Allowance methodology — CECL

- **ASC 326-20 — Financial Instruments — Credit Losses (CECL)**. FASB Accounting Standards Codification Topic 326, Subtopic 20, Measurement of Credit Losses on Financial Instruments. The binding US GAAP measurement standard for the allowance for credit losses on loans and held-to-maturity securities. Anchors section 5 (allowance methodology oversight). https://asc.fasb.org/Topic&trid=2127064 [verify URL].
- **Interagency Joint Statements on CECL implementation** (December 19, 2016; March 2017; July 2019; FRB SR 19-8 [verify SR number]) [verify final post-CECL state — early statements covered transition; current operative statement may differ]. Anchors second-line governance expectations on CECL methodology, qualitative-factor framework, and sensitivity analysis. https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr1908.htm [verify URL] for the July 2019 statement.
- **Interagency Frequently Asked Questions on the New Accounting Standard on Financial Instruments — Credit Losses** (most recent revision) [verify current revision date]. Answers technical questions on CECL methodology, qualitative-factor governance, and model-validation expectations; cite by FAQ item number. https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/topics/cecl.htm.

## Insider credit — Regulation O (Federal Reserve Board)

- **12 CFR Part 215 — Regulation O — Loans to Executive Officers, Directors, and Principal Shareholders of Member Banks**. Applies to insured depository institutions through cross-references in OCC, FRB, and FDIC rules. Anchors section 6 (Reg O scope). https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-II/subchapter-A/part-215.
  - **§215.2** — Definitions, including executive officer, director, principal shareholder, related interest, and extension of credit.
  - **§215.3** — Extensions of credit (scope and computation).
  - **§215.4** — General prohibitions; arms-length terms; prior board approval requirement for extensions of credit aggregating more than the §215.4(b) threshold.
  - **§215.5** — Additional restrictions on loans to executive officers (residence, education, other-purpose limits).
  - **§215.7** — Lending limit applicable to insiders.
  - **§215.8** — Records of member banks (insider-credit recordkeeping).
  - **§215.9** — Reports by executive officers.
  - **§215.11** — Disclosure of credit from member banks to executive officers and principal shareholders.

## Affiliate credit — Regulation W (Federal Reserve Board)

- **12 CFR Part 223 — Regulation W — Transactions Between Member Banks and Their Affiliates**. Implements sections 23A and 23B of the Federal Reserve Act. Anchors section 6 (Reg W scope) for credit transactions with affiliates. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-II/subchapter-A/part-223.
  - **§223.11** — Quantitative limits (10% of capital stock and surplus per affiliate; 20% aggregate across all affiliates).
  - **§223.12** — Collateral requirements for covered transactions.
  - **§223.13** — Low-quality-asset prohibition.
  - **§223.14** — Permissible covered transactions on attribution and aggregation.
  - **§223.16** — Exemptions from §223.11 quantitative limits.
  - **§223.42** — Sister-bank exemption [verify section].
