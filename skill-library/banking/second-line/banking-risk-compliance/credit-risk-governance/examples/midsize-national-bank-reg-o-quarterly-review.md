# Example: Midsize national bank quarterly Reg O review with director-related credit transactions

Public-source-derived. No named institution. Roles only.

## Engagement context

A midsize national bank (asset size band 10B-50B, approximately $18B in total consolidated assets, OCC-regulated) runs a quarterly Reg O review under §215.8 recordkeeping and §215.9 reporting cadence. In the current quarter, two new credit transactions involve related interests of two directors and one new transaction involves a related interest of an executive officer. The Reg O officer requests the second-line review pack ahead of the audit committee meeting and a future OCC examination cycle. Source posture is public-plus-firm-policy-plus-evidence; the access population is the audit committee, the chief credit officer, the chief compliance officer, the Reg O officer, and outside counsel.

## Executive summary

- Reg O posture clean for the quarter; insider population current; aggregate lending-limit headroom remains at 18% of unimpaired capital and surplus.
- Three new insider transactions this quarter: two director-related-interest commercial credit lines (one $4M, one $8M), both prior-board-approved per §215.4(b); one $250k executive-officer residential mortgage on principal residence within §215.5 specific limits.
- One open observation on the related-interest determination memo for the $8M transaction (light on the §215.2(n) related-interest analysis on the corporate ownership chain).
- Reg W not applicable this quarter; affiliate inventory current.
- Heightened Standards not applicable; asset size below $50B.
- Overall confidence medium; high on Reg O scope (clean recordkeeping and reporting), medium on related-interest determination depth.

## Section 7: Second-line challenge

Sample-based challenge of all three insider transactions confirms documentation completeness against the §215.4 arms-length-terms framework. One observation: the related-interest determination memo for the $8M transaction is light on the §215.2(n) related-interest analysis (corporate ownership chain not traced through the intermediate holding entity). Action item for the Reg O officer: refresh the related-interest determination memo before audit committee.

This is a quarterly Reg O cycle, not a full credit-administration review. Sections 1 through 5 run as a one-line posture confirmation:
