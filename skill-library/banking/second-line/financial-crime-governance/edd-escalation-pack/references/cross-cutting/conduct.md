# Conduct cross-cutting overlay — edd-escalation-pack

When the scope flags conduct, or when a PEP / wealth / private-banking / correspondent profile makes conduct exposure material, load this overlay alongside `references/source-anchors.md`. PEP and high-risk-customer profiles intersect with conduct risk in ways that produce material findings the EDD committee should see.

## Why conduct sits inside an EDD pack

The EDD pack is built on a financial-crime frame, but high-risk customer profiles frequently carry conduct exposure on top of the financial-crime exposure: a senior foreign political figure's family member with adverse-procurement reporting carries reputational and conduct exposure beyond the AML / sanctions read; a high-net-worth private-banking customer with a complaint pattern around suitability or sales practice carries market-conduct exposure; a vulnerable customer pulled into a high-risk relationship through a third party carries vulnerable-customer-conduct exposure. The EDD committee's decision is not only an AML decision; it is a relationship decision the firm makes against the full risk surface.

## Conduct-side anchors

The conduct overlay does not displace the financial-crime anchors; it adds the conduct lens.

- **FCA conduct guidance** (where the firm has UK nexus). The FCA's senior managers and certification regime, the Consumer Duty (where in scope of the customer relationship), and conduct-of-business sourcebook framing carry conduct expectations that bear on a high-net-worth or high-risk customer relationship from the UK side. [verify specific FCA handbook references and dates per the customer's jurisdictional nexus]
- **FFIEC BSA/AML Examination Manual** treatment of relationship behaviour as a risk driver [verify section]. The manual's framing of customer-behaviour-as-risk-driver supports the EDD pack's conduct read on US-regulated institutions.
- **CFPB UDAAP framework** (where retail harm is in scope). Where the high-risk relationship intersects with a retail-customer base or a vulnerable customer, the UDAAP frame supports the conduct lens. [verify CFPB UDAAP examination manual section]
- **FINRA suitability and Reg BI** (broker-dealer customer relationships). Where the EDD escalation involves a wealth or prime-brokerage relationship, suitability and Reg BI carry the conduct frame for product recommendation against the customer's profile. [verify rule references]
- **State insurance department conduct guidance** (where applicable). Where the EDD escalation involves a covered-insurance customer, state-level conduct guidance and NAIC market-conduct standards apply on top of the BSA frame. [verify references per state]

## Conduct indicators the EDD pack reads

- **PEP family member or close associate adverse-conduct exposure.** A PEP or family member named in an adverse procurement, anti-bribery, or political-corruption report carries conduct exposure beyond the financial-crime read; the pack flags the exposure with the source, date, materiality, and linkage confidence.
- **Complaint-pattern indicators tied to the relationship.** A high-net-worth or private-banking customer with a recurring complaint pattern (suitability, mis-sale, pressure-sale, advisor-conflict pattern) carries conduct exposure that intersects with the EDD frame; the pack reads the firm's complaint record on the relationship where that record is available.
- **Vulnerable-customer overlap with high-risk profiles.** A vulnerable customer (under the FCA framing or the firm's vulnerable-customer policy) pulled into a relationship through a third party (family member, advisor, fiduciary) carries elevated conduct exposure; the pack reads the relationship structure and the third-party role.
- **Sales-practice and suitability exposure for private banking and wealth segments.** Where the EDD trigger includes product mis-match, leverage levels inconsistent with the customer's documented profile, or recurring sales-practice exposures on the relationship, the conduct lens surfaces a finding alongside the financial-crime finding.
- **Market-conduct exposure for capital-markets EDD.** Where the customer's trading pattern carries market-conduct exposure adjacent to the financial-crime read (manipulation-adjacent patterns, layering on lit venues, wash-trade-adjacent patterns), the conduct lens flags the exposure.
- **Foreign-procurement and anti-bribery exposure.** Where the customer's relationship structure or transactional flow intersects with public reporting on foreign procurement, anti-bribery investigation, or political-corruption exposure, the conduct lens flags the exposure with the source.
- **Reputational exposure on the customer's connected parties.** Where connected parties (beneficial owners, signatories, key counterparties) carry adverse-conduct exposure independent of the customer, the pack flags the connection.

## How conduct findings flow into the pack

Conduct findings sit in section 8 of the pack (Conduct-risk indicators) with the indicator type, evidence, severity, and the regulator-anchor reference. Conduct findings can also raise the linkage confidence on the derogatory-information section (section 3) and the PEP / sanctions-adjacency posture (section 4); the pack notes the cross-section bearing.

Conduct findings can elevate the EDD committee's decision options: a customer with material conduct exposure on top of a marginal financial-crime exposure may warrant retention with conditions that the pure financial-crime read would not call for, or may move the committee toward escalating to relationship-exit deliberation. The pack frames the conduct contribution explicitly so the committee sees the seams.

## Boundary

The conduct overlay does not turn the EDD pack into a conduct review. The pack still sits on the BSA / AML / sanctions frame. The conduct lens surfaces findings that bear on the EDD decision; conduct-only findings without a financial-crime nexus belong in the firm's conduct-review machinery, not in the EDD pack.

## Common pitfalls in the conduct read

- Folding conduct in as a label without an indicator-with-evidence ("PEP-adjacent reputational exposure" without a source, date, or materiality).
- Treating commercial-PEP-screening-tool reputational tags as evidence; the pack reads the underlying source.
- Importing complaint patterns without linkage to the customer relationship (a firm-wide complaint trend is not a customer-specific finding).
- Over-reading vulnerable-customer status; the conduct lens applies where vulnerability bears on the EDD relationship, not as a default flag.
- Stating UK conduct anchors where the firm has no UK nexus; load the conduct anchors that apply to the firm's regulator constellation.
